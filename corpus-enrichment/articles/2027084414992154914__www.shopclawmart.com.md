About
Stop writing Klaviyo emails from scratch. This skill gives your AI agent everything it needs to build, send, and automate email marketing that actually looks like your brand.

Here is what is inside:

Brand-matched email components. 11 production-ready HTML blocks — headers, heroes, product cards, testimonials, CTAs, footers, and more. Fill in one config file (brand.yml) with your colors, fonts, and logo. Every email your agent builds will match your brand automatically.

10 automation blueprints. Welcome series, post-purchase, abandoned cart, winback, VIP loyalty, review requests, cross-sell, browse abandonment, birthday, and sunset flows. Each one includes timing, copy examples, subject lines, and the exact Klaviyo API payload to deploy it.

Copy that converts. Subject line formulas, hook frameworks, CTA patterns, and proven email copy structures. Your agent does not just build — it writes emails people actually open.

Full Klaviyo API reference. 15 reference docs covering campaigns, flows, segments, profiles, events, SMS, push notifications, reporting, catalogs, and more. Every endpoint, every parameter, with copy-paste curl examples.

Brand onboarding in 60 seconds. Give your agent a URL. It scrapes your website, extracts your colors, typography, and voice, and populates brand.yml automatically. No manual config needed.

Built by operators running real e-commerce email programs. Not a tutorial — a production system.

- 11 email components — header, hero, product card, grid, testimonial, social proof, image+text, CTA, divider, footer, text block
