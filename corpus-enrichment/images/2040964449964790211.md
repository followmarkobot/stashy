Anj's life scaling laws #1

• Take life seriously

• But not so seriously that you forget how to have fun and what makes life worth living
  ○ We have a very short amount of time on earth - don't take it for granted

• Work on fun projects with humans you enjoy hanging out with
  ○ Empirically, this seems to have scaled predictably well for me

• The most important people in this class aren't the professors or the speakers
  ○ It's the people around you
    ■ Invest in these relationships
  ○ Genuinely see how special and lucky you all are to be alive at this moment in history
  ○ Obsess over the things you love - this is how you scale yourself asymmetrically - obsessions, love, trust - these things don't scale within large organizations

• Maybe go to the real coachella while you still can!
